# Theology and scope

This project originated in a conservative Independent Fundamental Baptist pastoral search. It initially serves small Baptist and similar congregational churches especially well, while allowing other properly recorded governance arrangements. Extensibility is not a reason to erase its theological origin or imply that different polities make identical claims.

The initial qualification framework uses 1 Timothy 3:1–7 and Titus 1:6–9, preserving the source worksheet’s 26 entries and references. Contemporary descriptive headings help readers discuss the passages; they are not presented as a new Scripture translation. Each church records its own working meaning, evidence, disqualifying patterns, and interpretive questions before adoption.

The church remains responsible for its adopted doctrinal statement. The application does not settle disputed theology, marital/family interpretations, denominational boundaries, or ministry convictions. Church convictions, ministry fit, and personal preference remain distinct from the biblical framework, doctrine, and governance.

The optional starter preset declares a congregational call and committee model. It remains suggested until each value is reviewed and copied locally; it cannot amend governing documents. The source church’s composition, thresholds, staffing arrangements and calendar are not universal defaults.

Pastoral/theological reviewers should review generalized qualification descriptions, adapted source questions, polity language, interim safeguards, and pastoral-search philosophy. Qualified professionals should review legal, financial, employment and safety wording in a church’s actual circumstances. The public feedback preview is not an independently vetted denominational standard. Contributors must identify doctrinal/polity assumptions and must not silently rewrite a church’s declared identity.
