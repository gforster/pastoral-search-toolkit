# Community conduct

Treat contributors, churches, candidates, and families with dignity. Discuss evidence and ideas honestly, with patience and charity. Do not harass, threaten, demean, or disclose private church or candidate information. Disagreement about doctrine or polity is possible without misrepresenting another person’s convictions.

Keep issue reports and examples fictional or properly anonymized. Use the private reporting guidance in SECURITY.md for sensitive matters. Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content and limit participation when needed to protect people and the project. Report conduct concerns through a private maintainer channel when available; otherwise request a private channel without posting sensitive details.
